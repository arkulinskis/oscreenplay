From "what if" to FADE OUT

Five rooms, one project. Go in order or jump around; everything stays connected.

  1. Spark

    Generate a story abstract. Click any word to swap it, lock the ones you love, and develop it into a project in one click.

  2. Cast

    Want, need, wound, lie, flaw, fear, and Big Five personality. Ask how each character would react to a betrayal, a windfall, a declaration of love.

  3. Web

    A living relationship map: rivals, debts, secrets, love. Tension shows as weight; lonely characters get flagged.

  4. Two timelines

    The chronology (what happened, backstory included) against the storyline (the order we tell it). The braid shows every flashback.

  5. Pages

    A real screenplay editor: Enter and Tab do the formatting. Draft pages from your scene cards, export to Fountain, print to PDF.

Questions

Does it use AI to write?

No. Every suggestion comes from rules, story templates and a hand-built library of ideas: archetypes, wounds, flaws, conditions, twists, scene recipes. You do the writing; OScreenplay does the bookkeeping, the formatting and the nagging.

Is it free?

Yes. Create as many screenplays and sparks as you like.

Who can read my scripts?

Only you. Projects are private to your account. The sample scripts are the only ones everybody can open (read-only).

Can I take my script elsewhere?

Yes. Export to Fountain (opens in Final Draft, Highland, WriterDuet, Fade In and others) or print a properly formatted PDF. You can import Fountain too.

I've never written a screenplay. Is that a problem?

That's who the Learn page is for: format in ten minutes, the main structures, character craft, and a scene-by-scene masterclass. Every field in a project explains itself.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, from spark to pages. A laptop is nicer for long writing sessions.
